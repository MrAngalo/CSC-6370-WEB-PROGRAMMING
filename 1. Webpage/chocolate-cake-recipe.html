Chocolate Cake

This chocolate cake is soft, simple, and feeds eight people.

Preheat the oven to 350 degrees before you mix the batter.

Let the cake cool before you cut it, or it will fall apart.

Ingredients

  1. 2 cups of flour
  2. 1 cup of sugar
  3. 1/2 cup of cocoa powder
  4. 2 eggs
  5. 1 cup of milk
  6. 1/2 cup of oil
  7. 1 tablespoon of baking powder

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees.
  2. Mix the eggs, milk, sugar, and oil.
  3. Add the flour and the cocoa powder and stir.
  4. Add the baking powder last and mix gently.
  5. Pour into a greased pan and bake for 35 minutes.
  6. Let it cool, then serve.
