Pudding

This pudding is creamy, sweet, and takes about 20 minutes to make.

Keep the heat low so the milk does not burn at the bottom of the pan.

Chill it for at least two hours before serving.

Ingredients

  1. 2 cups of milk
  2. 1/2 cup of sugar
  3. 3 tablespoons of cornstarch
  4. 1 teaspoon of vanilla

Steps

  1. Mix the sugar and the cornstarch in a pan.
  2. Pour in the milk and stir until smooth.
  3. Cook on low heat, stirring, until it thickens.
  4. Turn off the heat and add the vanilla.
  5. Pour into bowls and chill for two hours.
